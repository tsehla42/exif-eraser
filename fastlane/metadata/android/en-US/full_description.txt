<p>ExifEraser is a modern, permissionless image metadata erasing application for Android. It strips EXIF, IPTC, XMP, ICC profiles, and other metadata from your images — protecting your privacy before sharing.</p>

<b>Why remove metadata?</b>
Images often contain hidden data like GPS coordinates, device info, camera settings, and timestamps. ExifEraser removes all of this so you can share photos without leaking personal information.

<b>Supported Formats</b>
• JPEG — ICC Profile, EXIF, Photoshop Resources, XMP/ExtendedXMP
• PNG — ICC Profile, EXIF, XMP
• WebP — ICC Profile, EXIF, XMP

<b>Metadata Viewer</b>
View complete metadata for any image before or after processing. Metadata is organized into expandable groups (EXIF, IPTC, XMP, ICC, etc.) with a Raw/Pretty toggle to switch between human-readable and raw tag values. Remove metadata directly from the viewer with one tap.

<b>No Permissions Required</b>
ExifEraser uses Android's Storage Access Framework (SAF) — it never asks for storage or camera permissions. You select files, it processes them. No network access, no tracking.

<b>Themes</b>
• Light, Dark, and AMOLED (pure black for OLED screens)
• System theme follows your device setting
• Dynamic Colors (Material You) on Android 12+

<b>File Naming Options</b>
Choose how processed files are named: Random UUID, original Image ID, processing timestamp, or EXIF date with fallback chain.

<b>Built With</b>
Jetpack Compose, Material3, Hilt, metadata-extractor, and exif-interface-extended.
