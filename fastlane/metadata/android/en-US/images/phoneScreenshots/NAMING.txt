Screenshot Naming Convention
============================

Name your screenshot files as plain sequential numbers:

  1.png
  2.png
  3.png
  ...

Rules:
- Format: PNG or JPEG only (.png, .jpg, .jpeg)
- No prefixes, no zero-padding, no underscores
- Just the number, then the extension
- Recommended: 2-5 screenshots
- Height/width ratio should be at most 2:1

These files are used by F-Droid, IzzyOnDroid, and the Orion Store.
All three pull from this same directory.
